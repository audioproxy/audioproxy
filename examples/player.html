Serve this file over http://localhost:… — for example ruby -run -e httpd . -p 8097 from this directory. Opening it as a file:// or data: URL gives the page an opaque origin, and Chrome refuses to let a non-secure origin reach the loopback address space. The failure shows up as a CORS error that has nothing to do with the proxy.
